# Balance (Getbalance)

> Balance provides financial infrastructure for B2B commerce: embedded digital trade credit, B2B buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) at checkout, multi-rail B2B payments (ACH, wire, credit card, global), marketplace payouts, and order-to-cash automation (invoicing, collections, cash application). Merchants and marketplaces offer net payment terms to business buyers while Balance automates the entire invoice-to-cash workflow with real-time credit intelligence and autonomous accounts receivable.

This file was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Balance web surface. The developer/API reference is gated behind a request form (ReadMe-hosted); the production API is served from https://api.getbalance.com.

## Products
- Digital Trade Credit — instant trade credit, up to net 90 days
- B2B BNPL — embedded buy-now-pay-later financing at checkout
- B2B Payments — ACH, wire, credit card, and global payment processing
- Marketplace OS — multi-vendor payment and payout solutions
- Order-to-Cash Automation — invoicing, collections, and cash application
